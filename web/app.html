Arm ● to record; press L to listen without arming. PC and input tracks can be recorded or monitored together. Click a clip or a MIDI motif to select it; Del removes it, Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes the last edit. S splits a take at the playhead; Ctrl/Cmd+C copies it and Ctrl/Cmd+V pastes it there. MIDI tracks play piano, bass or guitar notes drawn in the piano roll. Each take has a MIDI button that extracts its melody into an instrument track.
